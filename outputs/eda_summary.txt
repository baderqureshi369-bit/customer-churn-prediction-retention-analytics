================================================================================
KEY VERIFIED EDA FINDINGS
================================================================================

1. Overall Dataset Baseline:
   The baseline customer churn rate across all 7,043 customers was 26.54% (1,869 churned vs 5,174 retained).

2. Contract Commitment as Primary Differentiator:
   Month-to-month contract holders exhibited a 42.71% churn rate (1,655 churned out of 3,875), compared to
   11.27% for One year and 2.83% for Two year contracts, demonstrating an inverse relationship with commitment term.

3. Early Tenure Vulnerability:
   New customers (0–12 months tenure) showed the highest churn rate at 47.44% (1,037 churned customers),
   which steadily decreased to 28.71% (13–24 mos), 20.39% (25–48 mos), and 9.51% (49–72 mos).

4. Elevated Fiber Optic Churn Rate:
   Fiber optic internet subscribers experienced a 41.89% churn rate (1,297 churned out of 3,096),
   compared to 18.96% for DSL and 7.40% for customers with No internet service.

5. Protective Service Add-ons Association:
   Subscribers without OnlineSecurity (41.77% churn rate) or without TechSupport (41.64% churn rate)
   had almost 3x higher churn rates compared to those with active security (14.61%) or tech support (15.20%).

6. Payment Method Disparity:
   Electronic check was associated with the highest churn rate at 45.29% (1,071 churned customers),
   whereas automated payment methods (Bank transfer: 16.71%, Credit card: 15.24%) exhibited lower churn.

7. Monthly Charge Disparity:
   Churned customers paid an average of $74.44/month compared to $61.27/month for retained customers
   (an observed difference of +$13.17/month). Churn peaked in the $70.01–$90 monthly charge band (37.80%).

8. Demographic & Family Structure Patterns:
   Senior citizens showed an elevated churn rate of 41.68% (vs 23.61% for non-seniors), while customers
   without partners (32.96%) or dependents (31.28%) showed higher churn than those with family ties.
   Gender exhibited no practical difference (Female: 26.92%, Male: 26.16%).

9. High-Risk Compound Segment (Contract × Internet):
   The combination of Month-to-month contracts with Fiber optic internet had a 54.61% churn rate
   and accounted for 1,162 churned customers (62.17% of all dataset churn).

10. Maximum Churn Volume Concentration:
    A single micro-segment — Month-to-month | Fiber optic | 0–12 Months tenure — concentrated
    643 churned customers (churn rate: 70.20%), representing 34.40% of total dataset churn from 916 customers.
